Meet Nisha Kashyap

Founder of TheHumanWorksCo — 25+ years leading global HR, now dedicated to early-stage founders.

Founder's Note

"After 25+ years in global HR — leading teams, supporting M&A, partnering with C-suite leaders, and building People functions from scratch — I realised something: startups need senior HR expertise long before they can afford it."

Founders were moving fast, building incredible products, but struggling with the people foundations that make scale sustainable.

So I founded TheHumanWorksCo with one clear purpose: to help early-stage businesses scale confidently by powering the people engine behind the product.
