FAQ

Questions founders ask

What is a Fractional Head of People?

An experienced people leader — the judgement of a full-time Head of People or Chief People Officer — working with you a few focused days a month, at a fraction of the cost of a full-time executive.

Who do you work with?

Founders, CEOs and leadership teams at Pre-Seed to Series B startups and growing SMEs — usually from first hire up to around 150 people — plus GCCs establishing or scaling teams in India.

Can you build our people systems from scratch?

Yes. Hiring, onboarding, contracts and policies, performance, pay structure and your HR tooling. You end up with a working people function that's documented and yours to keep.

How is this different from an HR consultant?

A consultant hands you a report. I do the work — building the systems, running the hard conversations, coaching your managers — as part of your team.

Can you help with a one-off project?

Yes. Hiring pushes, restructures, compliance reviews, culture work or an annual people plan — scoped as a fixed project with a clear deliverable.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the days per month you need. Most founders start small on a rolling monthly retainer and scale as they hire. You'll get a clear number after the strategy session.
